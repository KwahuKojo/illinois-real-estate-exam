Essential Illinois & National Real Estate Exam Formulas & Rules

1. Illinois State & County Real Estate Transfer Tax State Rate = $0.50 per $500 (or fraction thereof) of Net Consideration (Purchase Price − Assumed Mortgages). Always round UP to the next whole increment! Most Illinois counties add $0.25 per $500 (total $0.75 per $500).
2. Illinois Property Tax Assessment & Proration in Arrears Outside Cook County, Assessed Value = 33.33% (1/3) of Fair Market Value. Equalized Assessed Value (EAV) = Assessed Value × State Multiplier. Because Illinois taxes are paid IN ARREARS, at closing the seller is DEBITED and the buyer is CREDITED for the days the seller owned the home during the current tax year.
3. Illinois Homestead & Recovery Fund Statutory Numbers Homestead Exemption = $15,000 individual / $30,000 married couple. Recovery Fund Cap = $25,000 per transaction / $100,000 aggregate per licensee. Escrow deposit deadline = Next business day after contract acceptance. Record retention = 5 years. Broker renewal = April 30 of even years (45 hrs post-license for 1st renewal; 12 hrs CE thereafter).
4. Income Capitalization Approach ("IRV" & GRM) NOI = Potential Gross Income − Vacancy/Collection Loss + Other Income − Operating Expenses (NEVER deduct mortgage P&I or income taxes).
Value = NOI ÷ Cap Rate • Cap Rate = NOI ÷ Value • Value = Monthly Gross Rent × GRM.
5. Cost Approach Formula Property Value = (Reproduction/Replacement Cost New − Accrued Depreciation) + Land Value. Remember: Land NEVER depreciates! Straight-Line Depreciation = (Effective Age ÷ Total Economic Life) × Building Cost New.
6. Rectangular Survey & Area Conversions 1 Township = 36 Sections = 36 sq. miles = 23,040 acres.
1 Section = 640 acres = 1 sq. mile (5,280 ft × 5,280 ft).
1 Acre = 43,560 sq. ft. • 1 Mile = 5,280 linear feet.
7. Mortgage Points, LTV & Seller Net Proceeds 1 Discount Point = 1% of the LOAN AMOUNT (not purchase price).
Required Sale Price for Target Net = (Desired Net + Mortgage Payoff + Fixed Closing Costs) ÷ (1 − Commission Rate).
